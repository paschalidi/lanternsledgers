# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Values-led small business owners, founders, charities and CICs (UK, around Bristol) who need their finances kept in order but don't want (or can't afford) a full-time finance director. They arrive feeling varying degrees of money-dread: behind on bookkeeping, unsure if they're profitable, dreading year-end. They want a real person they trust, not an account manager.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Lanterns & Ledgers, the fractional finance practice of Rhyannan Hall. Success = a visitor feels relief ("someone on my side who speaks plain English") and books a chat. Secondary: convey competence, warmth and calm at every scroll.

## Brand Personality

Warm, plain-English, illuminating. A calm lantern in the finance fog. Voice is first-person ("I keep the operations running"), never corporate ("we leverage"). Emotions to evoke: relief, trust, clarity. Three words: Warm, Trusted, Clear.

## Anti-references

- Corporate accounting/Big-4 coldness: navy-slate stock photos, jargon, formality
- Fintech neon/dark-mode clichés and "disrupt" energy
- Medicinal SaaS dashboards, identical icon-card grids
- Money-dread tropes: spreadsheets-as-chaos imagery used without warmth

## Design Principles

1. **Plain English in design too** — every section reads like Rhyannan talking; no mystery, no jargon, no dread
2. **Show the person** — a practice of one; the design should feel like a person's careful hand, not an agency's template
3. **Lantern light, not spotlight** — soft illumination, generous whitespace, quiet confidence; never loud or salesy
4. **Warmth carried by accent + type + imagery** — orange as fill/glow, ember reserved for CTAs, cream for panels only
5. **Calm under motion** — animations ease out gently and respect reduced motion; nothing bounces or races

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (sage-dark on off-white, navy on paper), large text ≥3:1 (orange only at large sizes, never body text on light bg). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Semantic HTML, labelled form controls, visible focus states.
